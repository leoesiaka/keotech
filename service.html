SERVICE 01

Fuel Station Design & Approval

Complete design and approval of retail filling stations, mega stations and modular stations — from concept through detailed engineering, regulatory approval and construction supervision.

Who it is for

Fuel station owners, oil marketing companies, investors and real estate developers.

What we deliver

  • Concept and detailed engineering design — civil, structural, mechanical and electrical.
  • 3D plant design and engineering documentation.
  • Equipment specification and layout optimisation for tanks, dispensers, pumps and forecourt automation.
  • NMDPRA Approval to Construct and Licence to Operate.
  • EIA, Fire Service certification, town planning and Weights & Measures support.
  • Safety, compliance and environmental documentation.
  • Construction supervision, commissioning, staff training and post-commissioning support.

Client benefits

  • Modular builds target 20–40% savings versus conventional construction.
  • Modular construction target of 8–12 weeks versus 4–6 months for conventional construction.
  • Approvals can be progressed in parallel.
  • Double-walled tanks, leak detection, overfill and cathodic protection.
  • Ex-rated equipment in hazardous zones.
  • Expansion interfaces for LPG, CNG and EV modules.
What makes this service different

Kaotech maps modular components to conventional regulatory requirements and engages NMDPRA early so modular systems can be reviewed and approved without unnecessary rework.

How services become projects

A structured path from first briefing to operating asset.

The service lines connect through a disciplined delivery model designed to keep engineering, approvals, procurement, construction and commissioning aligned.

01

Briefing & Scoping

Initial meeting to define facility type, location, budget and project requirements.

02

Assessment

Desktop research, field verification, site viability scoring, feasibility review and risk register development.

03

Concept & Detailed Design

Civil, structural, mechanical, electrical and automation design, including 3D plant design and P&IDs.

04

Regulatory Approvals

Pre-application engagement with applicable approvals, including NMDPRA, EIA, fire, town planning and permits.

05

Procurement

Equipment specification and sourcing, with local fabrication and off-site modular manufacture where suitable.

06

Construction Supervision

Site works, installation and systems integration supervised against approved drawings.

07

Testing & Commissioning

Hydrostatic and systems testing, commissioning, regulator inspection and Licence to Operate activities.

08

Operate & Support

Staff training, post-commissioning support and digital monitoring.
